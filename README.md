# 🛰️ **Smart Tourist Safety Monitoring & Incident Response System**  
### Using **AI, Geo-Fencing, and Blockchain-based Digital ID**

> An intelligent tourism-safety ecosystem leveraging **AI**, **blockchain**, and **geolocation** to ensure real-time protection, secure identification, and rapid emergency response for travelers.

---

## 🚩 **Overview**

The **Smart Tourist Safety Monitoring & Incident Response System** is a next-generation platform designed to safeguard tourists through **real-time monitoring**, **AI-based anomaly detection**, and **blockchain-secured digital identities**.  
It bridges the gap between **travelers, law-enforcement agencies, and tourism departments** to create a **safer, smarter travel experience**.

---

## 🧠 **Key Features**

### 🔐 **Digital Tourist ID Platform (Blockchain)**
- Generates **tamper-proof digital IDs** for tourists during check-in (airports, hotels, check-posts).  
- Stores **KYC details, itinerary, and emergency contacts** securely on a blockchain ledger.  
- IDs automatically **expire** after the trip duration.

### 📱 **Smart Tourist Mobile App**
- AI-based **Tourist Safety Score** calculated using travel behavior and area sensitivity.  
- **Geo-fencing alerts** for entry into high-risk or restricted zones.  
- **SOS Panic Button** instantly shares **live location** with nearest police unit & family.  
- Optional **real-time tracking** for families or authorized authorities.

### 🤖 **AI-Driven Anomaly Detection**
- Detects **abnormal movement patterns**, **sudden drop-offs**, or **prolonged inactivity**.  
- Automatically flags possible **distress or missing cases** for rapid response.

### 🗺️ **Tourism Department & Police Dashboard**
- Real-time **heatmaps** of tourist clusters and high-risk zones.  
- Access to **digital ID records, alert history, and last known locations**.  
- Automated **E-FIR generation** and incident logging.
